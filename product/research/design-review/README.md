# Design review

Two reviews of lablet's design, run on 2026-09-24 after phase 3a and before phase 4, by multi-agent workflows. Both are kept as delivered, so their numbering and their claims are those of the day. What was decided from them is in `decisions.md`, 2026-09-28, and the parity research in [../parity/](../parity/) later confirmed some of those decisions and contradicted others.

| Document                         | Covers                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [first-pass.md](first-pass.md)   | Four reviewers judging against the documents. Eight medium findings survived. Superseded by the second pass, which found the documents were the wrong yardstick                          |
| [second-pass.md](second-pass.md) | Eight reviewers judging against the goals, three verifiers per finding, a completeness critic. Nine high and twenty-four medium findings, and eleven decisions only the owner could make |
